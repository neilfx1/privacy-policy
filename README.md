## At a glance

- Your study data stays on your device unless you enable **optional iCloud sync**.
- I do not receive or have access to your scores, answers or study progress.
- The app contains **no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs**.
- If you email support, I receive the information you choose to send.

## 1. Your study data

The app stores the following information on your device so you can save your progress:

- Scores and answers
- Saved study and exam sessions
- Flashcard progress
- App preferences

The app does not require you to create an account with me and does not send your study data to a server operated by me.

## 2. Optional iCloud sync

**iCloud sync is optional and switched off by default.**

If you enable it, completed study and exam results and flashcard progress are stored in your own Apple iCloud account and synchronised between your devices.

Sessions still in progress and appearance preferences remain on the device where you set them.

I cannot access your private iCloud data. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

> You can turn off sync in the app’s Settings. Turning it off does not delete data already stored in iCloud.

## 3. Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

I do not sell your personal information or use your study activity for advertising.

Apple may process information when you use the App Store, iCloud or optional device diagnostics. Those services are governed by Apple’s privacy settings and policies.

## 4. Resetting and deleting your data

### Resetting scores and flashcard progress

You can reset your scores and flashcard progress in the app’s **Settings**.

- **With iCloud sync enabled:** the reset is synchronised when a connection is available.
- **With iCloud sync disabled:** the reset is applied to iCloud when you next enable sync.

Resetting progress leaves saved sessions and preferences in place.

### Deleting the app

Deleting the app removes its local app data. It does not automatically delete the separate iCloud copy.

You can manage the app’s iCloud data through your device’s iCloud storage settings. Data retained on another device may sync again if you continue using the app with iCloud enabled.

## 5. Contacting support

If you email me, I receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send.

I use these to:

- Respond to your enquiry
- Investigate reported problems
- Review feedback and question corrections

Support emails are handled through **Gmail**, which is provided by Google and subject to [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

I keep support messages only as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting me.

> **Please do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials or other sensitive information.**

## 6. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy if the app’s features or data-handling practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
